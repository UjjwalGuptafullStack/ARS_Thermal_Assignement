Processing Method

Daytime:

Use ST_B10.
Apply the official Landsat scaling factors.
Convert Kelvin to Celsius.
Mask clouds, cloud shadows, cirrus, snow, and invalid pixels.

Nighttime:

Use thermal band B10.
Convert digital numbers to radiance.
Apply Landsat 8 thermal constants K1 and K2.
Convert radiance to brightness temperature in Celsius.
Apply QA masking.

Important: the nighttime product is being reported as Brightness Temperature (BT), not Land Surface Temperature, because we have not applied emissivity correction.
